Why BRL1?

Stablecoins have revolutionized the global financial landscape and have proven to significantly reduce friction in cross-border payments involving the Brazilian market.

BRL1 provides a digital asset pegged to the Real, reliable and designed to boost liquidity, trading, and payments smoothly in both domestic and global economies.

BRL1 is also a market infrastructure solution, allowing secure, transparent, and frictionless transactions between platforms in the Brazilian on-chain economy.
